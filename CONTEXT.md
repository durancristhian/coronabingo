# Coronabingo

Coronabingo lets people play bingo together in shared rooms.

## Language

**Room**:
A shared space with a host, players, assigned cards, and game options. One room can be used for successive games.

**Game**:
One play session in a room, with its own called numbers and card marks.
_Avoid_: Room when referring to a single play session.

**Restart**:
The transition to preparation for another game in the same room, with a fresh draw and fresh card marks.

**Host**:
The player who directs the game and controls its called numbers.
_Avoid_: Admin without qualification, event organizer.

**Room setup**:
The preparation of a room's player list, host, cards, and game options before play.
_Avoid_: Site administration.

**Player**:
A participant with assigned bingo cards in a room. The host is also a player.

**Card**:
A bingo card assigned to a player, containing numbers the player can mark during a game.
_Avoid_: Ticket in user-facing English when referring to a bingo card.

**Called number**:
A number recorded by the host as drawn in the current game and shared with the room's players.

**Card mark**:
A player's selection of a number on their cards. Marks are distinct from the room's called numbers.

**Number board**:
The shared display of numbers 1–90 and their called state.

**Online draw**:
A mode in which the app randomly selects the next number for the host.

**Physical draw**:
A mode in which numbers are drawn outside the app and the host records them on the number board.

**Room code**:
An emoji sequence used to access protected room functions, including the host's cards when host protection is enabled.
_Avoid_: Account password.

## Historical language

**Charity event**:
A retired fundraising workflow whose event organizer reviewed registrations and donation receipts before assigning participants cards. Event organization is distinct from hosting a room's game.
_Avoid_: Event without qualification when it could mean an analytics event or a game action.
